# Changelog

## Build 3 (1.0.1) - 2026-11-08

- More filters in Movies and Series: language, release year, minimum rating, and genre and director for series.

## Build 2 (1.0.1) - 2026-11-07

- Fixed the empty catch-up list, and video that kept playing after leaving a player that was still starting.
- Fixed sources that could not be deleted after being removed during a sync.
- Parental controls: sources now need the PIN, and locked series leave Keep watching and the Series panel.
- Fixed poster search for titles ending in HD, 4K or a year, and guide programmes shown twice after moving.
- Less background work when scrolling big libraries. Uninstalling on Windows now offers to remove saved logins.

## Build 1 (1.0.0) - 2026-11-07

- First release, for Windows and Linux.
- Live TV, movies and series from M3U and Xtream Codes sources, with a guide, Home recommendations and automatic failover.
- Mouse wheel volume, remembered volume, new player keys, Start over, next episode autoplay, picture size and a sleep timer.
- Search across everything, favourite movies and series, hidden and PIN locked categories, preferred languages, catch-up, reminders and update alerts.
